# Half Lens — Digital Brand Translation

## Source reviewed

All 19 pages of `Half Lens Brand Guidelines.pdf` were rendered and inspected visually. The guide is the identity authority for this redesign.

## Official identity behavior

- The logo contrasts a complete circular lens with a sharp half-lens cut.
- The construction is angular, geometric, and repeatedly resolves around a diagonal approximately 45 degrees.
- Use the supplied official logo asset only. Never redraw, stretch, outline, recolor outside the approved palette, rearrange pieces, or place it over insufficient contrast.
- On navy use the official white/reversed logo. On ice or white use the official navy logo when available.

## Typography

- The brand guide specifies Cairo.
- Cairo is the primary Arabic and bilingual interface family.
- Arabic display copy uses confident 500–600 weights with safe glyph line-height; body copy uses 400–500.
- Latin technical labels remain secondary and compact. They never become the main brand voice.
- Hierarchy is created through scale, width, and whitespace—not by making every label uppercase or every heading oversized.

## Web geometry

- **Complete frame:** a full circle or uninterrupted media field represents the completed outcome.
- **Half frame:** a 45-degree cut, incomplete rule, or split crop represents the starting idea.
- **Completion:** scroll, focus, or hover may align the two halves, open a crop, or complete a thin rule.
- Use geometry at scene boundaries, media reveals, focus states, and one or two signature controls. Do not notch every box.

## Pattern and framing rules

- Thin outline structures may be derived from the logo proportions.
- Patterns remain large, sparse, and low-contrast.
- Media should be hard-cropped and dominant; framing should clarify the work rather than decorate it.
- Negative space is structural. Empty regions must support alignment or pacing, not exist as arbitrary dead zones.

## Image treatment

- Prioritize real Half Lens films, campaign frames, equipment, crew, sets, and finished client work.
- Preserve natural cinematic grading. Do not apply expensive animated full-screen filters.
- Use navy or ice fields to separate chapters; mint may mark a focus point or completion state.
- Avoid generic stock offices, unrelated technology, AI people, or imagery sourced from reference sites.

## Motion principles

- Motion feels like framing and editing: crop opens, split aligns, rule completes, next frame arrives.
- Use transform and opacity first; clip-path only for brief, measured reveals.
- Keep input responsive and reversible. No long catch-up, bouncing, decorative smooth-scroll hijacking, or animated blur.
- On reduced motion, compositions are immediately complete and all content remains readable.

## Never introduce

- Fuel's orange/teal identity, exact compositions, wordmark behavior, crosshairs, pricing, testimonial, or CEO card.
- Generic glassmorphism, neon gradients, excessive pills, random 3D objects, or software-dashboard conventions.
- Logo fragments that fail to resolve to the official mark or distort it.
