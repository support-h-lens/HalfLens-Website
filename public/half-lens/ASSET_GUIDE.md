# Half Lens public asset guide

Provide approved production assets using these paths and formats. The current site continues to use its existing verified files until replacements are supplied.

```text
public/half-lens/
  brand/
    logo-primary.svg
    logo-reversed.svg
    mark.svg
    pattern-outline.svg
  hero/
    poster-desktop.webp      1920x1080
    poster-mobile.webp       1080x1350
  projects/<project-slug>/
    poster.webp              1920x1080, sRGB
    preview.mp4              6–10s, muted, no audio autoplay
    gallery-01.webp          1600px+ long edge
  clients/
  behind-the-scenes/
```

- Keep filenames lowercase and version/hash media when replaced.
- Use WebP/AVIF for stills where visual comparison confirms quality.
- Preserve the official logo's clear space and never bake it into low-contrast imagery.
- Project assets must be owned or approved by Half Lens; do not hotlink social media or reference-site media.
